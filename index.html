Prepare all parts & check voltage ratings

Connect Solar Panel → MPPT Charge Controller

3
Connect MPPT → LiFePO4 Battery (match polarity + & -)
Ikabit ang MPPT sa Baterya (tandaan ang + at -)
4
Connect Battery → Buck Converter (adjust output voltage)
Ikabit ang Baterya sa Buck Converter (i-adjust ang boltahe)
5
Connect your load → Test & enjoy!
Ikabit ang gamit → Subukan at gamitin na!
