📖 Step-by-Step Build Guide

STEP 1 — Prepare All Components

🇬🇧 English:

Gather all components: Solar Panel, MPPT Charge Controller, Circuit Breaker, LiFePO4 Battery, Buck Converter, Wires, Fuse, Switch. Double-check all parts are complete and undamaged.

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Ihanda ang lahat ng kagamitan: Solar Panel, MPPT Charge Controller, Circuit Breaker, LiFePO4 Battery, Buck Converter, Kawad, Puse, Switch. Siguraduhing kumpleto at walang sira ang lahat.

STEP 2 — Connect Solar Panel to MPPT

🇬🇧 English:

Connect Solar Panel wires to MPPT Solar Input terminal. RED = Positive (+), BLACK = Negative (-). Match polarities carefully — wrong connection can damage the controller!

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Ikabit ang kawad ng Solar Panel sa Solar Input ng MPPT. PULA = Positibo (+), ITIM = Negatibo (-). Siguraduhing tama ang pagkakakabit — kapag binaligtad, maaaring masira ang MPPT!

⚡ STEP 3 — Install CIRCUIT BREAKER (CRITICAL SAFETY!)

🇬🇧 English:

Connect the Circuit Breaker between the MPPT and the Battery — on the POSITIVE (+) wire ONLY! This protects your system from short circuits and overload. Turn breaker OFF before connecting battery. Install a 10A–15A fuse as extra protection. Never skip this step!

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Ikabit ang Circuit Breaker sa pagitan ng MPPT at Baterya — sa POSITIBO (+) na kawad lamang! Pinoprotektahan nito ang sistema mula sa short circuit at sobrang karga. I-OFF muna bago ikabit ang baterya. Maglagay ng 10A–15A na puse bilang dagdag na proteksyon. Huwag na huwag lalakasan ang hakbang na ito!

STEP 4 — Connect Battery via Breaker to MPPT

🇬🇧 English:

With breaker OFF: Connect Battery → Circuit Breaker → MPPT Battery Output. Positive (+) through breaker, Negative (-) direct. Then turn breaker ON. The MPPT should light up and show battery voltage on its display.

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Naka-OFF ang breaker: Ikabit ang Baterya → Circuit Breaker → Battery Output ng MPPT. Ang Positibo (+) dadaan sa breaker, ang Negatibo (-) direkta. Pagkatapos ay i-ON ang breaker. Dapat umilaw ang MPPT at makita ang boltahe ng baterya sa display nito.

STEP 5 — Test & Verify Under Sunlight

🇬🇧 English:

Place the solar panel under direct sunlight. Check the MPPT display — it should show charging status and rising battery voltage. If not charging, recheck all connections and polarity.

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Ilagay ang solar panel sa direktang sikat ng araw. Tingnan ang display ng MPPT — dapat makita na nagcha-charge at tumataas ang boltahe ng baterya. Kung hindi nagcha-charge, suriin muli ang lahat ng koneksyon at polarity.

STEP 6 — Add Buck Converter & Output Devices

🇬🇧 English:

Connect Buck Converter input to battery or MPPT load output. Adjust output to 5V USB using a voltmeter. Now you can charge phones, lights, and other USB devices! Add a switch for convenience.

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Ikabit ang input ng Buck Converter sa baterya o sa load output ng MPPT. I-set ang output sa 5V USB gamit ang voltmeter. Pwede na itong pang-charge ng cellphone, ilaw, at iba pang USB devices! Maglagay ng switch para madaling patayin at buksan.

✅ STEP 7 — Secure, Protect & Maintain

🇬🇧 English:

Secure all wires with tape or cable ties. Keep everything dry and protected from rain. Check connections monthly. Keep panel clean. Always turn the Circuit Breaker OFF before doing any wiring!

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Isiguro ang lahat ng kawad gamit ang tape o cable ties. Ilagay sa tuyong lugar at protektahan mula sa ulan. Suriin ang koneksyon buwan-buwan. Panatilihing malinis ang panel. Laging i-OFF ang Circuit Breaker bago gumawa ng anumang koneksyon!

⚠️ Important Safety Tips

🇬🇧 English

  • ⚡ Circuit Breaker is MANDATORY — Always install on Positive (+) line
  • 🔴🔴 Never reverse polarity — Red=+, Black=- — Double-check every connection
  • 💧 Keep all components dry — Rain causes short circuits
  • 🔥 Use correct wire gauge — 18AWG minimum for short runs
  • 🔋 LiFePO4 is safe but still dangerous — Don't short circuit the battery terminals
  • 🛡️ Always turn breaker OFF before connecting or disconnecting wires
  • 👀 Monitor temperature — If anything gets hot, disconnect immediately

🇵🇭 Tagalog

  • ⚡ MANDATORY ang Circuit Breaker — Laging ikabit sa Positibo (+)
  • 🔴🔴 Huwag na huwag babaliktarin ang polarity — Pula=+, Itim=- — Suriing mabuti ang bawat koneksyon
  • 💧 Panatilihing tuyo ang lahat — Ang ulan ay nagdudulot ng short circuit
  • 🔥 Gumamit ng tamang kapal ng kawad — 18AWG o mas makapal
  • 🔋 Ligtas ang LiFePO4 pero delikado pa rin — Huwag ikabit ang + at - ng baterya
  • 🛡️ Laging i-OFF ang breaker bago magkabit o magtanggal ng kawad
  • 👀 Subaybayan ang init — Kung may uminit, patayin agad at alisin ang koneksyon
