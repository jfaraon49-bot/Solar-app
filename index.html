📖 Step-by-Step Build Guide

STEP 1 — Prepare Components

Gather the solar panel, appropriately rated controller, battery, protection, converter, wires and connectors.

STEP 2 — Connect Solar Panel

Verify polarity and connect the panel according to the controller manual.

STEP 3 — Install Protection

Use correctly sized DC-rated overcurrent protection.

STEP 4 — Connect Battery

Follow the manufacturer's wiring sequence and polarity markings.

STEP 5 — Test

Check controller indicators and measured voltage.

⚠️ Important Safety Tips

🇬🇧 English

  • ⚡ Use correctly rated protection.
  • 🔴 Verify polarity.
  • 💧 Keep components dry.
  • 🔥 Use appropriately sized wire.
  • 🛡️ Disconnect power before servicing.

🇵🇭 Tagalog

  • ⚡ Gumamit ng tamang fuse o breaker.
  • 🔴 Suriin ang polarity.
  • 💧 Panatilihing tuyo ang components.
  • 🔥 Gamitin ang tamang wire size.
  • 🛡️ I-disconnect ang power bago mag-service.

❓ Beginner FAQ

Can I connect any 12V panel to any 12V battery?

No. Check panel voltage/current, controller input range and battery charging requirements.

Do I need a fuse or breaker?

Overcurrent protection is an important part of a safe design.

Can this power a refrigerator or air conditioner?

Not as a simple beginner USB/12V project. Larger appliances need a properly designed system.
