📖 Step-by-Step Build Guide

STEP 1 — Prepare All Components

🇬🇧 English:

Gather all components: Solar Panel, MPPT, Circuit Breaker, LiFePO4 Battery, Buck Converter, Wires, Fuse, Switch.

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Ihanda ang lahat ng kagamitan: Solar Panel, MPPT, Circuit Breaker, LiFePO4 Battery, Buck Converter, Kawad, Puse, Switch.

STEP 2 — Connect Solar Panel to MPPT

🇬🇧 English:

Connect Solar Panel → MPPT Solar Input. RED = Positive (+), BLACK = Negative (-). Match polarities carefully!

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Ikabit Solar Panel → Solar Input ng MPPT. PULA = Positibo (+), ITIM = Negatibo (-). Siguraduhing tama ang polarity!

⚡ STEP 3 — Install CIRCUIT BREAKER (CRITICAL!)

🇬🇧 English:

Connect Circuit Breaker between MPPT and Battery — on POSITIVE (+) wire ONLY! 10A–15A fuse. Never skip this!

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Ikabit Circuit Breaker sa pagitan ng MPPT at Baterya — sa POSITIBO (+) lang! Maglagay ng 10A–15A na puse. Huwag lalakasan!

STEP 4 — Connect Battery via Breaker to MPPT

🇬🇧 English:

Breaker OFF → Battery → Breaker → MPPT Battery Output. Turn ON — MPPT should light up.

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Naka-OFF ang breaker → Baterya → Breaker → Battery Output ng MPPT. I-ON — dapat umilaw ang MPPT.

STEP 5 — Test Under Sunlight

🇬🇧 English:

Place panel in direct sun. Check MPPT — should show charging status & rising voltage.

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Ilagay ang panel sa araw. Tingnan ang MPPT — dapat nagcha-charge at tumataas ang boltahe.

STEP 6 — Add Buck Converter & Output Devices

🇬🇧 English:

Connect Buck Converter → Set to 5V USB. Charge phones, lights, USB devices!

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Ikabit Buck Converter → I-set sa 5V USB. Pwede nang pang-charge ng cellphone at iba pa!

✅ STEP 7 — Secure, Protect & Maintain

🇬🇧 English:

Secure wires, keep dry, check monthly. Always turn breaker OFF before wiring!

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Isiguro ang kawad, panatilihing tuyo, suriin buwan-buwan. Laging i-OFF ang breaker bago magkabit!

⚠️ Important Safety Tips

🇬🇧 English

  • ⚡ Circuit Breaker is MANDATORY — Positive (+) line only
  • 🔴🔴 Never reverse polarity — Red=+, Black=-
  • 💧 Keep all components dry — Rain causes short circuits
  • 🔥 Use 18AWG+ wire — Don't use thin wires!
  • 🛡️ Always turn breaker OFF before connecting wires

🇵🇭 Tagalog

  • ⚡ MANDATORY ang Circuit Breaker — Sa Positibo (+) lang
  • 🔴🔴 Huwag babaliktarin ang polarity — Pula=+, Itim=-
  • 💧 Panatilihing tuyo — Ang ulan ay nagdudulot ng short circuit
  • 🔥 Gumamit ng makapal na kawad — 18AWG o mas makapal
  • 🛡️ Laging i-OFF ang breaker bago magkabit ng kawad
