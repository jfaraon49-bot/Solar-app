📖 Step-by-Step Build Guide

STEP 1 — Prepare All Components

Gather the solar panel, appropriately rated charge controller, battery, overcurrent protection, converter, wires, connectors and switch. Tagalog: Ihanda ang lahat ng components at siguraduhing compatible.

STEP 2 — Connect Solar Panel to MPPT

Connect the panel to the controller's solar input and verify polarity before connection. Tagalog: Suriing mabuti ang positive at negative polarity.

⚡ STEP 3 — Install Overcurrent Protection

Install the correctly sized fuse or DC-rated breaker according to the controller, battery, wire size and system design. Tagalog: Huwag basta gumamit ng generic na rating.

STEP 4 — Connect Battery to Controller

Follow the charge controller manufacturer's wiring sequence and polarity markings.

STEP 5 — Test Under Sunlight

Place the panel in sunlight and check the controller's charging indicators and measured voltage.

STEP 6 — Add Buck Converter & Output Devices

Confirm the converter input range and output setting. Measure output before connecting a USB device.

✅ STEP 7 — Secure, Protect & Maintain

Secure wiring, protect components from water, inspect connections and disconnect power before servicing.

⚠️ Important Safety Tips

🇬🇧 English

  • ⚡ Use correctly rated overcurrent protection.
  • 🔴 Verify polarity before every connection.
  • 💧 Keep components dry.
  • 🔥 Use appropriately sized wire.
  • 🛡️ Disconnect power before wiring or servicing.

🇵🇭 Tagalog

  • ⚡ Gumamit ng tamang fuse o breaker.
  • 🔴 Suriin ang polarity bago magkabit.
  • 💧 Panatilihing tuyo ang components.
  • 🔥 Gamitin ang tamang wire size.
  • 🛡️ I-disconnect ang power bago mag-service.

🔧 Troubleshooting for Beginners

Start with the simplest checks. Disconnect power before changing wiring.

SymptomPossible causesBeginner checks
Controller is offBattery disconnected, protection open, reversed polarity, low battery.Check manual, battery connection, polarity and protection.
Battery is not chargingLow sunlight, panel issue, controller configuration.Check panel conditions and controller display.
USB output unstableConverter overloaded, wrong voltage, poor connection.Disconnect load and verify converter output.
Fuse/breaker opens repeatedlyShort circuit, overload, wrong rating.Do not repeatedly reset it; find the fault first.

📚 Solar Glossary

Volt (V)

Electrical potential.

Ampere (A)

Electrical current.

Watt (W)

Power; in simple DC cases watts ≈ volts × amps.

Watt-hour (Wh)

Energy used or stored over time.

MPPT

Controller method that tracks a useful panel operating point.

LiFePO4

Lithium iron phosphate battery chemistry.
