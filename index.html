📋 Step-by-Step Setup

Step 1 — Prepare Panel ⚡
Ikabit ang solar panel sa lugar na may direktang sikat ng araw.
Mount the panel facing the sun (south, 30° tilt). Ensure no shade.
Step 2 — Connect MPPT Controller 🔌
Ikonekta ang solar panel sa MPPT charge controller (PV terminals).
Panel → MPPT (PV side). Observe polarity (+/-).
Step 3 — Connect Battery 🔋
Ikonekta ang LiFePO4 battery sa MPPT (Battery terminals).
Battery → MPPT (Batt side). MPPT regulates charging automatically.
Step 4 — Add Load / Devices 📱
Ikonekta ang buck converter o devices sa load terminals ng MPPT.
Use buck converter to step down 12V → 5V for phones/LED lights.
Step 5 — Test & Monitor ✅
Check kung nagcha-charge at gumagana ang mga devices.
MPPT shows voltage & charging status. Keep wires short & secure.

⚠️ Safety Tips

  • Always connect battery FIRST before panel if MPPT allows.
  • Observe correct polarity — reverse = damage!
  • Use proper gauge wire — thicker = better for long runs.
  • LiFePO4 is safer than Lead-Acid — still avoid overcharging.
