Step-by-Step Guide

Step 1 — Prepare Components

EN: Gather Solar Panel, LiFePO4 Battery, MPPT Controller, Buck Converter, Wires, Fuse, Switch.

TL: Ihanda ang Solar Panel, LiFePO4 Baterya, MPPT Controller, Buck Converter, Kawad, Puse, Switch.

Step 2 — Connect Solar Panel to MPPT

EN: Positive (+) to Positive, Negative (-) to Negative. Do not reverse!

TL: Positive (+) sa Positive, Negative (-) sa Negative. Huwag baliktarin!

Step 3 — Connect Battery to MPPT

EN: Check polarity! Install fuse near battery positive terminal.

TL: Tignan ang polarity! Maglagay ng puse malapit sa positive ng baterya.

Step 4 — Test Charging

EN: Place under sunlight — MPPT should light up and charge the battery.

TL: Ilagay sa araw — Dapat umilaw ang MPPT at mag-charge ang baterya.

Step 5 — Add Buck Converter

EN: Connect battery output → set voltage (e.g., 5V USB) for phones/lights.

TL: Ikabit ang output → I-set ang boltahe (hal. 5V USB) para sa cellphone/ilaw.

Step 6 — Safety & Enclosure

EN: Add switch, protect connections, keep dry & ventilated. Avoid short circuit!

TL: Maglagay ng switch, protektahan ang koneksyon, ilagay sa tuyo. Iwasan ang short circuit!

Safety Tips

  • • Always check polarity (+ / -) — reversing damages parts!
  • • Use proper wire gauge — thicker wires for longer distance.
  • • LiFePO4 is safer — still avoid overcharging!
  • • Disconnect positive first when working on connections.
