📖 Step-by-Step Build Guide

STEP 1 — Prepare All Components

🇬🇧 English:

Gather the solar panel, appropriately rated charge controller, battery, overcurrent protection, converter, wires, connectors, and switch.

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Ihanda ang solar panel, charge controller, baterya, tamang fuse o breaker, converter, kawad, connectors, at switch.

STEP 2 — Connect Solar Panel to MPPT

🇬🇧 English:

Connect the panel to the controller's solar input. Verify positive and negative polarity before making the connection.

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Ikabit ang panel sa solar input ng controller. Suriing mabuti ang positive at negative polarity bago magkabit.

⚡ STEP 3 — Install Overcurrent Protection

🇬🇧 English:

Install the correctly sized fuse or DC-rated breaker according to the controller, battery, wire size, and system design. Do not automatically assume 10A or 15A is correct.

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Gumamit ng tamang fuse o DC breaker ayon sa controller, baterya, wire size, at disenyo ng system. Huwag basta gumamit ng 10A o 15A nang walang sizing.

STEP 4 — Connect Battery to Controller

🇬🇧 English:

Follow the charge controller manufacturer's connection sequence and polarity markings. Keep the protection device accessible.

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Sundin ang wiring sequence at polarity markings ng manufacturer ng charge controller. Panatilihing madaling ma-access ang protection device.

STEP 5 — Test Under Sunlight

🇬🇧 English:

Place the panel where it receives sunlight. Check the controller's charging indicators and measured voltage.

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Ilagay ang panel sa lugar na may sikat ng araw. Tingnan ang charging indicator at voltage reading ng controller.

STEP 6 — Add Buck Converter & Output Devices

🇬🇧 English:

Connect the buck converter only after confirming its input range and output setting. Measure the output before connecting a USB device.

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Ikabit ang buck converter pagkatapos tiyaking tama ang input range at output setting. Sukatin muna ang output bago magkabit ng USB device.

✅ STEP 7 — Secure, Protect & Maintain

🇬🇧 English:

Secure all wiring, protect components from water, inspect connections regularly, and disconnect power before servicing.

🇵🇭 Tagalog:

Isiguro ang lahat ng kawad, protektahan sa tubig, regular na inspeksyunin ang connections, at patayin ang power bago mag-service.

⚠️ Important Safety Tips

🇬🇧 English

  • ⚡ Use correctly rated overcurrent protection for the actual circuit.
  • 🔴🔴 Verify polarity before every connection.
  • 💧 Keep electrical components dry and protected from rain.
  • 🔥 Use appropriately sized wire for the actual current and distance.
  • 🛡️ Disconnect power before wiring or servicing.

🇵🇭 Tagalog

  • ⚡ Gumamit ng tamang fuse o breaker para sa aktuwal na circuit.
  • 🔴🔴 Suriin ang polarity bago magkabit.
  • 💧 Panatilihing tuyo at protektado sa ulan.
  • 🔥 Gamitin ang tamang wire size ayon sa current at haba.
  • 🛡️ Patayin at i-disconnect ang power bago mag-wire.

🔧 Troubleshooting for Beginners

Start with the simplest checks. Disconnect power before changing wiring.

Symptom Possible causes Beginner checks
Controller is off Battery disconnected, protection device open, reversed polarity, low battery. Check the controller manual, battery connection, polarity and protection device.
Battery is not charging Low/no sunlight, panel connection issue, controller configuration, battery protection. Check panel conditions and controller display; verify the panel is within the controller's input range.
USB output is unstable Converter overloaded, incorrect voltage setting, poor connection, undersized wiring. Disconnect the load and verify the converter output voltage before reconnecting.
Fuse/breaker opens repeatedly Short circuit, overload, wrong protection rating, wiring fault. Do not repeatedly reset it. Disconnect the system and find the fault before restoring power.

📚 Solar Glossary — Simple Definitions

Volt (V)

Electrical potential — think of it as the “pressure” pushing charge.

Ampere (A)

Electrical current — how much charge is flowing.

Watt (W)

Power. In simple DC cases: watts ≈ volts × amps.

Watt-hour (Wh)

Energy used or stored over time.

MPPT

A controller method that tracks a panel's useful operating point.

LiFePO4

Lithium iron phosphate battery chemistry with its own charging requirements and protection needs.
