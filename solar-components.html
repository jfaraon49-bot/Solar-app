📌 Important Notes:

  • LiFePO4 Battery: Longer life (4,000–6,000 cycles vs 500 for lead-acid), safer, lighter, and can be discharged deeper (80–90%). Always use with built-in BMS protection.
  • Prices: Based on current market listings (Aug 2026). May vary with promotions, COD fees, and shipping.
  • MPPT: Always choose an MPPT controller rated ≥120% of your panel's max current for safety.
  • Wire Gauge: Use thicker wire (lower AWG number) for longer distances — 12AWG minimum for solar panel connections.
  • Verification: Buy from sellers with 4.5+ star ratings and many reviews to avoid counterfeit parts.
